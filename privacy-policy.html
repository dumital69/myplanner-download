Politika privatnosti — MyPlanner

MyPlanner (BDPSoft) je aplikacija za planiranje zadataka. Ova politika objašnjava koje podatke prikupljamo, zašto, i kako ih štitimo.

Koje podatke prikupljamo

  • Email adresa — koristi se isključivo za prijavu (autentikaciju) i identifikaciju naloga preko Firebase Authentication servisa.
  • Status pretplate — informacija do kog datuma je nalog aktivan (polje „paidUntil"), koju administrator ručno upisuje nakon što proveri uplatu.

Šta NE prikupljamo

Svi vaši zadaci, podzadaci i beleške čuvaju se isključivo lokalno na vašem uređaju (u lokalnoj memoriji telefona). Ti podaci se nikada ne šalju na server niti su vidljivi razvojnom timu. Ako obrišete aplikaciju ili nalog, ti podaci se trajno brišu.

Plaćanje

Pretplata se trenutno plaća preko PayPal-a, direktno između vas i administratora. Aplikacija ne obrađuje niti čuva podatke o platnim karticama — PayPal transakcije su van naše kontrole i podležu PayPal-ovoj sopstvenoj politici privatnosti.

Gde se podaci čuvaju

Email i status pretplate čuvaju se na Firebase Realtime Database serveru (region Evropa — europe-west1), servis kompanije Google. Podaci se prenose šifrovano.

Deljenje sa trećim stranama

Ne prodajemo niti delimo vaše podatke trećim stranama u marketinške svrhe. Jedini spoljni servisi koje koristimo su Firebase (Google) za autentikaciju i PayPal za plaćanje.

Brisanje naloga

U svakom trenutku možete zatražiti brisanje naloga direktno kroz aplikaciju (opcija za brisanje naloga briše i sve lokalne podatke) ili slanjem zahteva na email ispod.

Deca

Aplikacija nije namenjena deci mlađoj od 16 godina i svesno ne prikupljamo podatke od dece.

Izmene ove politike

Ova politika se može povremeno ažurirati. Datum poslednje izmene naveden je na vrhu stranice.

Privacy Policy — MyPlanner

MyPlanner (BDPSoft) is a task planning app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Email address — used solely for login (authentication) and account identification via Firebase Authentication.
  • Subscription status — the date until which an account is active ("paidUntil" field), set manually by the administrator after verifying payment.

What we do NOT collect

All your tasks, subtasks, and notes are stored exclusively on your device (local storage). This data is never sent to a server and is never visible to the development team. Deleting the app or your account permanently deletes this data.

Payment

Subscriptions are currently paid via PayPal, directly between you and the administrator. The app does not process or store any card details — PayPal transactions are outside our control and are governed by PayPal's own privacy policy.

Where data is stored

Email and subscription status are stored on Firebase Realtime Database (Europe region — europe-west1), a Google service. Data is transmitted encrypted.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. The only external services we use are Firebase (Google) for authentication and PayPal for payment.

Account deletion

You may request account deletion at any time directly within the app (the delete-account option also removes all local data) or by emailing the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions: bdp69soft@gmail.com
